Open archive of schoolbook downloaders

One shelf for every schoolbook you own.

Folio gathers the free, open downloaders for the digital textbook platforms you already pay for — HUB Scuola, MyLim, bSmart, Zanichelli, Pearson, Sanoma and more — and runs as much of them as possible right here, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is stored. No account with us, ever.

I —

In-browser, right now

These platforms expose CORS and serve page data your browser can reach directly. Drop in your token (or, for HUB, your email and password — both work), pick your book, download a merged PDF. Everything stays on your machine.

HUB Scuola · Mondadori Education

HUB Young / Kids / Scuola

Log in with email & password or paste a session token. Browse your library, download any volume as one merged PDF.

Open downloader MyLim · Loescher

MyLim direct PDF

Paste your MyLim JWT. Your books are served as ready-made PDFs — search, filter out demos, one click to download.

II —

The rest of the shelf

The other platforms keep their APIs locked behind CORS — a browser alone can’t reach them, and no workaround is worth your credentials being mishandled. For each one there is a battle-tested CLI tool, copied from the shelf into this repo, ready to run on your machine.

Twelve publishers, one folder

  • bSmart · DiBooK (Laterza) · Educadhoc (Hachette)
  • Hoepli demo · Pearson eText · Sanoma
  • WSET Global · Zanichelli (Kitaboo + Booktab)

Every tool lives in its own folder, MIT-licensed, with the original README intact.

III —

Keep it small, keep it yours

Nothing to install

HUB and MyLim work right here on this page. No app, no account, no waiting room.

Nothing to trust

Your token and password never leave your browser. Nobody — including us — can see them.

Nothing to pay

The site is static, hosted free on GitHub Pages. Bandwidth is free. So are the tools: all MIT.

About the legal fine print
These tools create copies of books you already have licensed. Back up your own books, keep grades off the internet, and read the licence inside each tool. Folio itself is a directory: each tool keeps its own MIT licence and authors.

“Scarichiamo i libri, non le librerie” — download the books you own, not the libraries.
