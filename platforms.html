Nota —

Better methods, in one line each

What we found, comparing methods

  • In-browser > everything, when CORS allows. No install, credentials never leave your machine. This is the MyLim & HUB path.
  • One Python CLI, all of Italy: pdfgrabber handles Scuolabook, MyLim, Pearson (both), bSmart, HUB, MEE2, easyeschool, Zanichelli (both), Oxford, DiBooK, Raffaello in a single script.
  • Same DRM family everywhere. bSmart, Zanichelli Kitaboo and Pearson all use AES-CBC/RSA page encryption — crypto written once tends to transfer.
  • Node + better-sqlite3 & pdf-lib is the most reliable merge stack for HUB (SQLite chapter index → chapter zips → merged PDF).
  • pdfgrabber quality: perfect PDF & TOC on MyLim and Scuolabook; Pearson eText needs the Reader+ app’s T&C accepted once.

Recommendation

Keep two lanes open:

1. This site grows in-browser coverage platform by platform, as CORS (or your own free Worker) allows.

2. pdfgrabber as the power tool for the rest — one install, one command, every Italian service. Both lanes are MIT and free.

No downloader here is better than the others by much: they share the same ~4 building blocks (fetch pages, decrypt, render, merge). What differs is how much of that you’re willing to run in a browser tab vs. a terminal.
